# Plans

What you pay for: three Plans at flat prices with hard limits, chosen before your first App, and never a surprise bill.

Every Plan is a flat price per month with hard quotas. Nothing is metered: an App that reaches a limit is paused or its Deploy refused, with a message saying which limit and what to do, never an extra charge. Members of your Apps never pay. Prices are in US dollars, billed monthly.

## The Plans

### Starter

$5 a month, for one App, at its basemodo.app address or at [two domains of your own](https://basemodo.com/docs/domains) with HTTPS. A Starter App sleeps when nobody uses it.

You choose a Plan and add a card before your first App: until then, creating, deploying or restoring an App, or taking one in by Transfer, is refused ([`plan_needed`](https://basemodo.com/docs/errors#plan_needed)). Being a Member or an Editor of someone else's App needs no Plan.

### Pro

$18 a month, for a person with several Apps: up to five Apps, [workers](https://basemodo.com/docs/workers-and-cron) in two of them (Always On Apps, which never sleep), [your own domains](https://basemodo.com/docs/domains) with HTTPS (two per App), 3 GB of disk per App, Logs kept 7 days and Backups up to 14 days, a Backup of each App every hour too, and failed cron runs retried. A Pro Owner who needs more Apps or Always On Apps moves them to a Workspace.

### Workspace

$25 a month for two Seats, plus $5 a month for each Seat beyond them, for a team or a company, on one bill. A Workspace pays for the two Seats even while one person has joined, so the first colleague joins at no charge. It has everything in Pro, plus Apps owned by the Workspace (so they outlive the person who built them), three Always On Apps, two CPUs per App, [Workspace Visibility](https://basemodo.com/docs/sharing#workspace), [Admins](https://basemodo.com/docs/roles#admin), and an outbound network allowlist per App ([`network.allow`](https://basemodo.com/docs/manifest#network.allow)). A Workspace can grow past its Plan: each App beyond its five is an Additional App, $4 a month, and each Always On App beyond its three is $6 a month. Only an Admin adds one, and only after agreeing to its price: creating an App, accepting a Transfer, or restoring a deleted App beyond what the Workspace pays for, or deploying (or rolling back to) workers beyond its Always On Apps, first says the price and asks ([`charge_required`](https://basemodo.com/docs/errors#charge_required); `basemodo deploy`, `basemodo rollback` and `basemodo restore` wait for an Admin to approve it on basemodo.com, in their own browser, or take the approval with `--approval`). An Editor's or an agent's Deploy beyond them asks for the same approval, which only an Admin gives; a push from GitHub uses only those paid for and free ([`always_on_not_reserved`](https://basemodo.com/docs/errors#always_on_not_reserved)). Once they agree, the rest of the month is charged at once to the card on file, without moving the date the Plan renews, and each month after is on the invoice; a declined card adds nothing ([`card_declined`](https://basemodo.com/docs/errors#card_declined)). Deleting an App, or taking its workers away, lowers what is paid for, and the rest of the month is credited on the next invoice. An Admin can also reserve Seats, Additional Apps and Always On Apps ahead, on the billing page or with [`basemodo plan reserve`](https://basemodo.com/docs/cli/plan-reserve), so people join and the team creates Apps and deploys workers without asking: the Workspace pays for the larger of what is in use (the people who joined, the Apps beyond the Plan's) and what is reserved, and never for fewer than the two Seats its Plan includes. A reservation of Seats counts every Seat, those two among them: reserving ten bills eight at $5. A reservation is a floor, never a cap: nobody is ever kept from joining, and people join at no new charge until they outnumber the Seats reserved. Reserving more than is paid for says the price first and charges the rest of the month at once; reserving fewer lowers what is paid for, never below what is in use, and credits the rest of the month on the next invoice. Reservations end with the Plan. People can be invited by email, or join by themselves with an email on the company's verified domain. Someone invited joins or declines from the invitation's link; a declined invitation stays declined, and the verified domain does not bring them in, until an Admin invites them again.

Likewise, an Admin chooses the Workspace Plan on the Workspace's page before its first App ([`workspace_plan_needed`](https://basemodo.com/docs/errors#workspace_plan_needed)), as the last step of creating it or later from its billing page. The Workspace is bought whole there: before checkout the Admin sets how many Seats, Additional Apps and Always On Apps to pay for from the first month, with the monthly total in view, and checkout bills them; what is bought beyond what is in use is reserved. Seats start at the people who joined plus the invitations pending, never fewer than the two the Plan includes, and never go below the people who joined. Each person who joined the Workspace is a **seat**, Admins and Members alike; someone only invited is not one until they join, and someone taken out is not one any more. People you invite to a single App are not seats: they never pay, and neither does anyone else in the Workspace but the Workspace itself.

## What each Plan allows

| Plan | Price | Apps | Always On Apps | Custom Domains | Each App's Machine | Each App sends out | Logs kept | Backups kept | Failed cron runs retried |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $5 a month | 1 | none | 2 per App | 256 MB, 1 CPU, 1 GB | 2 GB a day, 20 connections a second | 7 days | 7 days | none |
| Pro | $18 a month | 5 | 2 | 2 per App | 512 MB, 1 CPU, 3 GB | 2 GB a day, 100 connections a second | 7 days | up to 14 days, and hourly ones 24 hours | 3, after 1, 2 and 4 minutes |
| Workspace | $25 a month for 2 Seats, plus $5 per Seat beyond them | 5, then $4 a month each | 3, then $6 a month each | 2 per App | 512 MB, 2 CPUs, 3 GB | 2 GB a day, 200 connections a second | 7 days | up to 14 days, and hourly ones 24 hours | 3, after 1, 2 and 4 minutes |

Apps, Always On Apps and custom domains count per Owner; the Machine's memory, CPU and disk are each App's. An App with a [worker](https://basemodo.com/docs/workers-and-cron) never sleeps, so it is an Always On App; every other App sleeps when nobody uses it. A Plan's custom domains are two for each App it allows, shared by all of the Owner's Apps.

## Limits per App

Each App's machine has a fixed share of memory, CPU and disk set by its Plan. An App that hits one is paused with the reason, and you are told by email and in your notifications. Upgrading applies at once.

## Abuse and limits

Every App shares Basemodo's outgoing addresses with every other App, so each Plan also caps what an App sends out: a bandwidth per day and new connections per second. An App over a cap is slowed down until the day ends (UTC), never charged; `basemodo status` says so (`throttle`, with why and until when) and you are told in your notifications. An App flooded with requests is held to lower request limits for an hour, the same way.

A new account is on **probation**: the first 14 days after it first pays, or after a Workspace is made. Probation has lower egress caps than any Plan. Your billing page shows the caps you are held to and when probation ends.

How much an App sends never pauses it by itself: sending far more than it usually does (an upload, a migration, a busy day) is only slowed down at its caps, and stays in its Usage for you to read. Basemodo watches for traffic that looks like abuse: connecting to thousands of hosts (scanning), looking up a mining pool or a command-and-control domain, hammering one host (alone or together with Apps of other accounts), deploying the same files as an App already stopped for abuse, or CPU busy for hours in an App with no worker (mining). Such an App is paused at once with the reason, so it sends nothing more, and a person at Basemodo reviews it; you are told by email. On the App's page, its Owner can tell them what it was doing, and change it until the review ends. Nothing deploys it meanwhile ([`app_under_review`](https://basemodo.com/docs/errors#app_under_review)). If nothing is wrong, it is resumed and you are told. Mining, a second App under review within 30 days, or a review during probation for anything but a busy CPU also suspends the account: every App paused and nothing new created or deployed ([`account_suspended`](https://basemodo.com/docs/errors#account_suspended)) until the review ends. Write to abuse@basemodo.com if you think a pause is a mistake.

## Logs, Backups and cron retries

Each App keeps its [Logs](https://basemodo.com/docs/cli/logs) 7 days on every Plan, up to 100 MB of lines a day on Starter and 200 MB on Pro and Workspace: past that, no more lines are kept until the day ends (UTC), and the Logs say so in one line. Each of its [Backups](https://basemodo.com/docs/data) is kept as long as its Owner's Plan says. On Pro and Workspace Basemodo also backs each App up every hour, each of those kept 24 hours, and a [cron run](https://basemodo.com/docs/workers-and-cron) that fails or times out is tried again, each retry waiting twice as long as the one before; the Logs say when the next try is. On every Plan, a cron run Basemodo itself could not start is tried again the same way. [`basemodo status`](https://basemodo.com/docs/cli/status) shows what an App has.

Each Backup is a full copy of the App's disk, so an App's Backups together take at most ten times its disk (30 GB on Pro and Workspace, 10 GB on Starter). Most Apps never come near it. One whose Backups would take more keeps, in this order, its latest hourly Backup, then its daily Backups (and those you took) from the newest back, then its other hourly Backups in the room left; the oldest go first. A full 3 GB disk that does not compress keeps its latest hourly Backup and its 8 newest daily ones. The App's Backups page lists the Backups it has.

A change of Plan reaches every App at once. Upgrading keeps up to 200 MB of Logs a day from then on, new daily Backups 14 days, and starts the hourly Backups. Downgrading keeps up to 100 MB a day from then on and stops the hourly Backups; a Backup already taken keeps the expiry it was taken with, so none goes early unless the smaller disk's cap lets the oldest go.

## When a server is lost

Each App runs on one of Basemodo's servers. If one is lost, Basemodo first makes sure it runs nothing any more, then brings its Apps back on another server, each from its latest [Backup](https://basemodo.com/docs/data): a Failover. Apps come back several at once, in this order: those that were awake first (Workspace Apps, then Pro, then Starter), then those that were asleep, in the same order. An App that was asleep comes back asleep and wakes on its next visit, as usual. Your notifications on basemodo.com say which Backup your App came back from. If your App's latest Deploy had not reached Basemodo's storage when the server was lost, your App comes back on the Deploy before it that had, and your notifications say so: deploy again (`basemodo deploy`) to bring the latest back.

What your App wrote after that Backup is lost with the server: up to an hour of data on Pro and Workspace, which back each App up every hour, and up to a day on Starter, which backs it up once a day. For now a person at Basemodo starts each Failover, so no time to come back is promised.

## Paying

Everything about money is on the billing page on basemodo.com: your Plan, your next invoice, your card and your invoices. [`basemodo plan`](https://basemodo.com/docs/cli/plan) shows your Plan and your next invoice from the terminal (`--workspace` for a Workspace's, as one of its Admins), and your agent has the same `plan` tool.

Every Plan renews monthly until you cancel it; the billing page says so, with the price, beside the button that pays.

- **Choosing a Plan** takes you to the payment provider's checkout, even with a card on file; it asks for your name and billing address, no tax ID. Your Plan starts as soon as it is paid: your first App can be created then, and Apps paused because a Plan ended resume where they were.
- **Changing Plan** happens on the payment provider's page (between Starter and Pro; a Workspace's Plan is not switched): **Manage your Plan** under Payment on the billing page opens it, and [`basemodo plan change pro`](https://basemodo.com/docs/cli/plan) does the same from the terminal. Moving up to Pro applies at once, and the rest of the month is charged then, prorated. Moving down to Starter applies from your next month, with nothing credited: you keep Pro until then. The payment provider's page shows what it charges before you confirm; nothing changes until you do, and Basemodo follows it. A Plan set to end is kept first ([`plan_ending`](https://basemodo.com/docs/errors#plan_ending)). If your Apps do not fit in the new Plan, the most recently created ones over its Quotas pause, with the Plan's limit as the reason (Apps, or Always On Apps), and you are told; they stay paused until you delete Apps or move to a bigger Plan, which resumes them where they were.
- **Your card** is changed on the payment provider's page, from the billing page. If it is declined, nothing changes ([`card_declined`](https://basemodo.com/docs/errors#card_declined)).
- **Invoices** are Link's: Basemodo is sold through Link, Stripe's merchant of record, and provided by Konnectia USA LLC. Link emails each receipt and Invoice with its PDF; Basemodo emails none. The billing page lists each one, with a link to its page at Stripe.
- **Your next invoice** is on the billing page before it is issued: its date, its amount and each of its lines (the Plan, the Workspace's Seats, a difference after a change).
- **A Workspace's seats**: each month's invoice bills the Workspace Plan, its two seats included, and each seat beyond them at $5. A seat beyond the two added or taken out during the month is prorated onto the next invoice, as a Plan change is: the rest of the month charged for the third person who joins (and each after), credited for someone taken out above two (never an invoice of its own). The second person to join adds nothing. An Admin is told this before it happens: inviting asks them to confirm what the seats will cost a month and about how much is added to the next invoice, removing someone says about how much is credited on it, and verifying the company domain says how many people who already signed in at it join now, what their seats add, and that anyone at it who signs in later joins and is billed the same way. Every join by the verified domain is a notification to each Admin on basemodo.com (not emailed). A Workspace without the Workspace Plan is charged nothing for who joins; choosing it bills the seats it has then. A Workspace's Plan, seats, card and invoices are on the Workspace's page, for its Admins; every Admin is told of its invoices on basemodo.com.

If the payment provider cannot charge your card after retrying, your Plan ends: your Apps pause with the reason, their data is kept 30 days, and choosing a Plan again resumes them ([`plan_ended`](https://basemodo.com/docs/errors#plan_ended)).

## Cancelling

Cancel any time on the payment provider's page, which **Cancel Plan** on the billing page opens (or from Link, which always lets you cancel); a Workspace's Plan is cancelled by one of its Admins. Your Plan stays until the end of the month already paid, and no further invoices come: you keep everything you paid for until then. Until that date, **Keep my Plan** on the billing page undoes it there. [`basemodo plan`](https://basemodo.com/docs/cli/plan) shows when a cancelled Plan ends.

You are told by email when you cancel, and again three days before the end (every Admin, for a Workspace). On that date the Plan ends as when the payment provider ends it: your Apps pause, their data is kept 30 days (and can be [downloaded](https://basemodo.com/docs/data#downloading-your-data) meanwhile), and choosing a Plan again resumes the Apps where they were. If your card fails after you cancelled, the Plan still ends on the same date. Cancelling never ends a Plan early and never refunds the month already paid.
